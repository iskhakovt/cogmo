Re-evaluate when RustFS hits a stable 1.0 — at that point compare against SeaweedFS (Apache 2.0, more mature, S3 gateway over a non-S3-native filer) and Garage (AGPLv3, Rust, geo-distributed focus) for our single-container test fixture. Keep Chainguard until at least one Apache-2.0 alternative is production-grade. No code change today; this is a calendar-watch item.
