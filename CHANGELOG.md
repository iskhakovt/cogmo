| 2026-04-11 | Crash recovery contract — summarization LLM call inside `compactMessages` wrapped in `step.run("summarize-prefix", ...)` so it's exactly-once on Inngest retry. The compaction orchestration (token counting, threshold decisions) deliberately stays non-durable: caching it would freeze the decision against stale `memory.recall` / profile inputs and force base64 image payloads into Inngest state. Replay regression test (`handle-message.replay.test.ts`) using `@inngest/test`'s `steps:` mechanism — proves cached durable steps don't re-execute their bodies (user/assistant inserts, summarization), and that the streaming section intentionally re-runs (canary for the non-durable contract). New `design/crash-recovery.md` documents the full durability map of `handle-message`, per-tool re-execution table, streaming dedup, and process-death edge case. `agents.md` Crash Recovery section now points to it. |
