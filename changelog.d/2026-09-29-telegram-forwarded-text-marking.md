Telegram forwarded messages are no longer read as the user's own words. Forwarded text, captions and voice-note transcripts carry a `forwarded` marking (origin kind, sender name, original send time) and reach the model, and the stored transcript the Observer reads, wrapped in `<forwarded_message from="…" origin="…" sent="…">`, with attribute values entity-escaped and the element's closing tag escaped in the body. A forwarded photo or document without a caption gets an empty element naming its sender, a forwarded `/command` goes to the agent as forwarded text instead of running, and auto-recall queries with the forwarded body alone. The web chat shows each forwarded message as a quote headed "Forwarded from …".
