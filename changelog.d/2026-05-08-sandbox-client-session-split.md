Sandbox public surface restructured around `SandboxClient<TState>` (factory) + `SandboxSession<TState>` (per-task handle), with discriminated session state, `SandboxCapabilities` flags, buffered `exec()` (size-capped via `SANDBOX_EXEC_BUFFER_LIMIT`), and `execStreaming()` (stream-based handle with `dispose()`). `LocalInProcessSandbox` becomes `LocalDockerSandboxClient`. New `tryResumeByTaskId(taskId)` collapses the lookup-and-attach sequence into one backend-owned operation; the local-Docker impl filters to `depth=0` so resume returns the root, not a child container. Coding orchestrators, skills tier-2 worker, and bootstrap migrated; behaviour unchanged.
