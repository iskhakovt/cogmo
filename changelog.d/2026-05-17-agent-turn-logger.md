Thread a per-invocation child logger (`logger.child({ runId, conversationId })`) through `runStreamingAgentLoop` / `runAgentLoop` via a new optional `turnLogger` field on `AgentLoopParams`. `handle-message` constructs the child at orchestrator entry and passes it into the loop; in-loop log emissions (history-invariants repair, iteration-limit warn, tool-round debug) route through it so every line inherits `runId` + `conversationId`. Foundation for upcoming Class C/D telemetry — joins structured logs to `conversation/degraded` / `conversation/errored` events by those two fields without per-emission ceremony. No behavior change: when `turnLogger` is absent the loop falls back to the module-level logger.
