**OpenRouter model ids get their limits from OpenRouter's own model list.** The model catalog refresh fetches `https://openrouter.ai/api/v1/models` beside LiteLLM's registry, and OpenRouter's list supplies the `openrouter/*` namespace that LiteLLM no longer publishes. An `<org>/<model>` id resolves to OpenRouter's entry for it first. Its context window is the smaller of the model's and its top provider's, and its output limit is the top provider's completion cap, under the same budget caps as every other entry. `MODEL_CATALOG_OPENROUTER_URL` points that fetch at a mirror, or `off` refreshes LiteLLM's registry alone. The bundled `data/litellm-models.json` is regenerated from both sources: 369 of the 470 OpenRouter ids it carried resolve from OpenRouter's list, and the 70 OpenRouter no longer lists and 85 entries LiteLLM has removed fall to the 128k / 4k default.
