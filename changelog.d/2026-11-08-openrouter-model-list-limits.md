**OpenRouter model ids get their limits from OpenRouter's own model list.** The model catalog refresh fetches `https://openrouter.ai/api/v1/models` beside LiteLLM's registry, and OpenRouter's list supplies the `openrouter/*` namespace that LiteLLM's registry lacks, so an `<org>/<model>` id resolves to OpenRouter's entry unless LiteLLM keys the exact id. Its context window is the smaller of the model's and its top provider's, and its output limit is the top provider's completion cap, under the same budget caps as every other entry. When OpenRouter's list can't be fetched or is unusable, the refresh keeps the previous catalog's `openrouter/*` entries beside LiteLLM's fresh ones and logs a warning. `MODEL_CATALOG_OPENROUTER_URL` points that fetch at a mirror, or `off` skips it. The bundled `data/litellm-models.json` is regenerated from both sources: of the 470 OpenRouter ids it carried, 369 resolve from OpenRouter's list and 28 through LiteLLM's keys, and the 70 OpenRouter no longer lists fall to the 128k / 4k default, as do 85 routing and embedding entries LiteLLM has removed.
