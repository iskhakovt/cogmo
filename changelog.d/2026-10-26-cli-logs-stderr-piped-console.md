**`cogmo` commands other than `serve` log to stderr**, so their stdout carries only their output (`cogmo model list | cut -f1` reads rows, not `database migrations applied`). `main.ts` calls `sendLogsTo("stdout" | "stderr")` before dispatching; the module-level `logger` writes through a router that opens the chosen stream there, so a misconfigured stream (pino-pretty missing under `NODE_ENV=development`) fails once at startup. A process that never chooses opens stdout on its first line. Both the production JSON destination and the dev pino-pretty transport follow the choice and get pino's config message.

**`pnpm console` handles piped input.** It reads lines with `for await`, sends each after the previous reply, and exits after the last reply rather than at EOF; it exits 1 when any line went unanswered. A first Ctrl+C shuts down cleanly and a second exits at once.
