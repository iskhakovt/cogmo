`DaytonaMock` WS replay now pauses at recorded `up` frames until the client actually sends a message, instead of emitting every recorded frame back-to-back. Without this, the recorded `close` (which encodes Daytona's exit reason in `close.reason`) raced ahead of the application's `sendInput`; the SDK's `handleClose` flipped `connected = false`, and the next `sendInput` threw `DaytonaConnectionError: PTY is not connected`. The fix matches the recording side, which captures every client→server message as an `up` frame in order — replay now treats them as the same checkpoints they were on the wire.

Pinned by `keeps WS open through SDK's sendInput, even when fixture has a trailing close` in `src/test/daytona-mock.test.ts`, which drives the real `@daytonaio/sdk` `PtyHandle` against a hand-crafted PTY fixture.
