The Observer reads corrections turn by turn, from a durable cursor: a `turn_observations` row per turn and phase, keyed on the turn's inbound cursor (design/observation.md → Slice 1a). Each fire discovers the answered turns no fire has observed, then extracts and applies one turn per step, oldest first, up to 10 per idle fire and 3 per `/reflect`. A turn's rule writes commit in the transaction that moves its row from `pending` to `applied`, so a correction counts once per turn that carries it: a re-run step finds the row applied and calls no model, a later fire never plans the turn again, and a concurrent apply retries onto `applied` and writes nothing. Before this, every fire re-read the whole transcript, so one old correction could be reinforced on each fire and promote a rule on a single turn's evidence.

Contradictions key on the turn: `steering_rules.contradicted_in_turn_id` records the turn whose contradiction reset a rule still learning, and a contradiction from any other turn, in the same conversation or another, retires it. A rule contradicted in two turns of one long chat now retires instead of resetting once.

Each turn's extraction gets the exchange before it as context, and an item counts only when its new `evidence` field quotes the turn's own user text, so a correction read in the context turn isn't counted again. Scheduled and pipeline turns are skipped as `not_user_speech`, a turn with no user text as `empty`. A failure the turn caused (a refusal, a cut-off reply, a parse failure past repair, a context-length or other unplaced 4xx) is counted on its row and fails the turn at the third attempt, or at once when it would recur; any other failure is the step's to retry and leaves the row as it was.

Migration `0081_turn_observations` creates the table and its enums, adds `contradicted_in_turn_id`, and starts each conversation's cursor where the transcript-era Observer stopped: turns the latest fire with a successful correction extraction read are `skipped` (`backfilled`), unread turns older than 30 days `skipped` (`aged_out`), and the rest are discovered as `pending` on the next fire. Each conversation-keyed contradiction record moves to its conversation's newest backfilled turn, and `contradicted_in_conversation_id` is cleared and kept, unread, for one release so a rollback still finds it.

Memory extraction still reads the whole transcript until it moves to turns (todo.md); the correction prompt's shape changed, so the Observer's integration cassettes re-record.
