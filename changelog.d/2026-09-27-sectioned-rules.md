`# Rules` renders in sections by authority: Always (operator rules, `safety` first), From your user, Learned from your user, and Channel defaults, under a line telling the model to follow the rule listed first when two conflict. Empty sections are left out, and within a section the narrower profile and channel scope comes first. `steering_rules.source` is the `steering_rule_source` enum, with `seed` for the channel defaults `seedChannelRules` writes: migration 0059 moves the seeded Telegram rows from `manual` to `seed`, and seeding checks only `seed` rows, so a user's channel-scoped rule doesn't stop it. The correction-learning eval's probe follows an active rule in 9 of 12 samples on this rendering (N=3), at least as often as the flat list's 4 of 7 (N=2, on the prompt before the turn context); every miss is the 100-word limit.
