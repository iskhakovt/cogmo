The Observer's correction-extraction and rule-consolidation prompts list existing steering rules under ordinal labels (`R1`…`Rn`) instead of their database ids. The model echoes a label, which the extractor and consolidator map back to the rule id; a label that names no listed rule is skipped with a warning. A reinforcement no longer depends on the model copying a 36-character UUID exactly, and the prompts are byte-stable across runs, so a recorded fixture can replay them.
