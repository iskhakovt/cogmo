The OpenAI-compatible adapter sends a reply's token cap as `max_completion_tokens` to OpenAI's reasoning models — the o-series and GPT-5 onward, by their bare ids — which reject `max_tokens` with a 400 (measured on `gpt-5-nano`, `gpt-5.4-nano`, `gpt-5.6-luna`, `gpt-6-luna`, `o3-mini` and `o4-mini`). A direct OpenAI row can serve those models. Every other model keeps `max_tokens`, including OpenRouter's `openai/…` slugs, which OpenRouter accepts it for (measured on `openai/gpt-5.4-nano` and `openai/gpt-6-luna`); some OpenRouter models and DeepSeek document only `max_tokens`. Live scenario D's OpenAI route runs on `gpt-5.4-nano`, where the follow-ups read 2,816 of 2,969 and of 2,990 prompt tokens with `prompt_cache_key`.
