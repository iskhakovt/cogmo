**Boot latency.** `bootstrapSandbox` was awaiting `reconcileCrashedInstances` synchronously on every `cogmo serve` start. On the local-docker backend that scans every container the daemon knows about — fast on a clean host, slow when the daemon is carrying many orphans from prior runs. On Daytona it was already a no-op, so the await cost was just symmetry overhead. Neither outcome blocks the first request: ongoing orphans are handled by the per-minute sandbox reaper (local-docker) and per-task TTL deletes (both backends). The boot pass only catches what the periodic reaper would catch on its next tick.

`scheduleReconcileCrashedInstances` now fires the reconcile in the background with structured logging on both fulfilment (warn when `orphansReaped > 0`) and rejection (error with `instanceId` and `backendLabel`). `cogmo serve` boot no longer waits on it.

Captured the broader boot-blocking policy as a `[confirmed]` section in `design/infrastructure.md` so the audit findings (MCP `start()` already a no-op, skill warm pool already lazy via `SkillRunnerImpl`, fast probes left blocking for operator-visibility reasons) don't have to be relitigated next time someone touches the bootstrap path.
