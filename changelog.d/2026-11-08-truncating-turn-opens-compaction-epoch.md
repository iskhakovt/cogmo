Append-only step 6: a turn whose Strategy 3 truncation or unstored summary rewrote its history opens a `compaction` epoch itself, so it strips the thinking before its row instead of replaying it under a prefix the rewrite changed.

- The epoch records `compaction` unless an earlier reason in the epoch rule applies, such as `summary` for a summary the turn stored. The turn's heads stay `compacted`, so the next turn, which reloads the full rows, opens another.
- The opening snapshot shows core memory as it is, so a change whose announcement the rewrite took out of view is in the prompt rather than announced again.
