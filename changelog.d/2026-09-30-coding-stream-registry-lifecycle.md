Coding progress streams have an owned lifecycle, and release their memory. `CodingStreamingRegistry` holds a task only while something is subscribed to it, and keeps no text of its own. The orchestrators publish through its `planStream` and `executeStream` handles; a publish to a task without subscribers is dropped, so a replayed step body or the verify phase holds nothing. `failed`, and an `execute_complete` reporting success, end the stream and release the task. A failed execute's `execute_complete` leaves the stream open for the `failed` that follows, so the progress message shows the failure reason. Tasks that end without a stream event (Revise or Cancel at the plan gate, a failure before the stream opened, reconcile) are released by `coding-stream-sweep`, a ten-minute cron, once two consecutive sweeps find the task terminal or gone in the database.
