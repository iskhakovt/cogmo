A skills-repo coding task whose pushed tip register's dry run refuses now fails before its PR opens. It fails `presubmit_refused`, with register's findings as the failure detail (at most 8 KiB, cut at a finding boundary with a count of the rest). A tip that doesn't contain `main` fails `presubmit_stale_base`, naming both shas. The new step `transition-presubmit-failed` is conditional on `verifying`, so a verify run already past `transition-pushed` when this ships carries on to its PR. Every other verdict still proceeds to the PR.

Nothing renders these failures from a run, so a new subscriber on `coding/task/failed` emits `coding/task/failure-notice` (one per task, under `failure-notice-<taskId>`). Telegram posts it into the task's conversation as the failure, its findings fitted to 4096 characters, and the pushed branch, so after a stale base the owner can merge `main` into the branch and register it by hand. `coding_task_failure_reason` gains the two values (migration 0090). A dry run's findings and details are masked before they are cut, and a cut text drops a trailing run that could hold a partial secret, so the stored verdict, the failure detail and the post carry no secret a cut hid from the mask.
