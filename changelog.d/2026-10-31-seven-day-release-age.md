**pnpm resolves to releases at least 7 days old.** `minimumReleaseAge` in `pnpm-workspace.yaml` is 7 days, matching Dependabot's npm `cooldown`, so a local `pnpm install` or `pnpm update` resolves to the same soaked versions a Dependabot PR would, and every pnpm install, CI's and the image build's included, re-checks the lockfile against it. The setting is non-strict: a range with nothing that old, such as a fresh security patch, resolves to the younger release, and the release then needs a `name@version` entry in `minimumReleaseAgeExclude` until it is 7 days old. `release-age.test.ts` holds the two settings equal.
