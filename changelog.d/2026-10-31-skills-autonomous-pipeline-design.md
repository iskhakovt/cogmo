`design/skills.md` specifies an autonomous skills pipeline: a skill draft state machine in which the user approves a manifest-level spec, mechanical verify gates and a Claude-plus-Codex reviewer panel stand where the approval tap was, and `approve` becomes opt-in per effect class. It also specifies a hardened tier-1 Pyodide child process, a token-injecting egress proxy as the one route out for both tiers, single-sourced pydantic and `ctx` contracts, run-failure classes with auto-disable, and the thirteen-slice plan in `todo.md` that builds them.
