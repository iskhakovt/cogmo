Daytona skill workers now use per-sandbox ephemeral `/skill-venvs/` instead of mounting the shared `cogmo-skills-deps-cache` Daytona Volume. Daytona Volumes are mountpoint-s3 FUSE mounts and lack the POSIX ops uv's cache + the populator's atomic-rename publish require (hardlinks, general rename, O_RDWR on existing files), which durably wedged skill registration with `EPERM` on every retry. Backends now advertise the choice via the new `SandboxCapabilities.depsCacheSharing` flag (`shared-volume` on Local-Docker, `per-sandbox` on Daytona); the skill runner and the `skill-venvs-reaper` cron consult the capability and omit the volume on per-sandbox backends, and `DaytonaSandboxClient.create` rejects `SessionSpec.depsCacheVolume` outright so wiring bugs fail fast. Per-worker cold populate on Daytona is seconds for typical deps and amortises across the worker's lifetime; cross-sandbox dedup via snapshot-per-lockfile bake is a future option tracked in `todo.md`.

**Upgrading on Daytona:** the existing `cogmo-skills-deps-cache` Daytona Volume becomes a no-op (never mounted) but stays in the org until manually removed. Run `daytona volume delete cogmo-skills-deps-cache` once after deploying, or remove via the Daytona dashboard. Daytona's per-org volume quota (100) is the only real impact; data itself is free.
