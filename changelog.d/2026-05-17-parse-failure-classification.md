Recover salvageable tool-arg JSON from streamed responses, and stop misclassifying genuinely unparseable arg streams as transient network failures. Both adapters (Anthropic `input_json_delta` accumulator, OpenAI-compatible `tool_calls[].function.arguments` accumulator) now run `jsonrepair` as a deterministic pre-pass before declaring the buffered chunks unparseable — trailing commas, missing braces within reason, and a few other common malformations are repaired and parsed without a turn-level repair attempt.

When even `jsonrepair` fails, the throw site now raises a new `ProviderProtocolError` (re-exported from `src/llm`) rather than the bare `SyntaxError` it used to leak. `FallbackLlmProvider`'s `isRetriableProviderError` predicate gets an `instanceof` guard so these errors propagate past the provider chain instead of triggering the status-less network-error heuristic and burning fallback budget on payloads no other provider would do better with. The in-loop classifier (PR 5 of agent-resilience) will own the recovery decision; this PR just stops the misclassification.
