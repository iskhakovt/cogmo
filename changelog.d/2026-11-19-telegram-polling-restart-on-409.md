Telegram polling now recovers from a 409 from `getUpdates`. A 409 means another poller or a webhook is using the bot token, and before this change polling stopped until cogmo restarted. The adapter now waits a backoff and calls `bot.start()` again, which also deletes a webhook. The backoff starts at 2.5–5 s and doubles up to 2.5–5 min, and a 10-minute healthy run resets it. A 401 (revoked or invalid token) and any other rejection still end polling, logged at error, and the process keeps running. `stop()` during a backoff cancels the restart.

The log lines changed, so an alert that matches on them needs updating. `telegram polling loop failed` is gone. `telegram adapter started` is now `telegram polling started` and logs on every start. A 409, a 401, any other rejection, and a loop that ends without `stop()` each log their own error line.
