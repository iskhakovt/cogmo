Telegram polling now recovers from a 409 from `getUpdates`, which means another poller or a webhook is using the bot token. Before, polling stopped until cogmo restarted. The adapter now waits a backoff and calls `bot.start()` again, which also deletes a webhook. The backoff starts at 2.5–5 s and doubles up to 2.5–5 min, and a 10-minute healthy run resets it. A 401 (revoked or invalid token) and any other rejection still end polling, logged at error, and the process keeps running.

The log lines changed, so an alert that matches on them needs updating. `telegram polling loop failed` is gone, and `telegram adapter started` is now `telegram polling started`, logged on every start. Every other supervisor line starts with `telegram polling: `. The 409, the 401, any other rejection, and a loop that ends without `stop()` log at error; a failure that settles while stopping logs at warn.
