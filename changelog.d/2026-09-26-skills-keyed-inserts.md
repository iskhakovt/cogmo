`ensureSkillsCodingRepo` writes the `skills` `coding_repos` row through a new `CodingStore.insertOrRecoverRepo` (`ON CONFLICT (name) DO UPDATE` with a no-op SET) instead of reading it and inserting on a miss. Two concurrent bootstraps used to leave the loser with `23505` on `coding_repos_name_unique`, which nothing retries; the keyed insert raises `40001`, and the transactor's retry lands on the existing row. `SkillStore.startOrRecoverRun` moves onto the same shape for consistency.

`.claude/rules/inngest.md` corrects its reason for that shape. On Postgres 18.6 under REPEATABLE READ, a keyed insert that conflicts with a row committed after its snapshot raises `40001` under `DO NOTHING` as well as `DO UPDATE` (`ExecCheckTupleVisible`), so either arm is safe for a concurrent loser; the hazard is a plain insert. `DO UPDATE` stays preferred as one statement for both arms. Store comments and design docs carry the corrected reason.
