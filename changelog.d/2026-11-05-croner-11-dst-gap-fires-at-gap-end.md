**croner 11: a scheduled task whose local time falls in a spring-forward gap fires at the end of the gap.** A daily `30 2 * * *` task in `Australia/Sydney` fires at 03:00 on the DST-start day, as Vixie cron does, rather than an hour after its set time at 03:30. Fall-back handling is unchanged (a fixed-time task fires once, at the first occurrence), and `computeNextRun` still returns the occurrence strictly after the previous fire. The release's other breaking changes (`startAt`, `dayOffset`, `previousRun()`) touch nothing cogmo calls.
