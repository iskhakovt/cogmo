`images/skills/.python-version` holds CI's skills-runtime tests on Python 3.14, the image's minor, where uv 0.13 would download 3.15. The image build syncs against it with downloads off, and `version-pins.test.ts` holds the skills FROM to it, so a base image on another minor fails in the unit tier and in the build.
