`createTransport` is a thin composition of per-namespace modules, behaviour unchanged.

- **Namespaces.** `transport/transport.ts` (~2.9k lines) is split into `transport/namespaces/<name>.ts`: sessions, boundary, conversations, chats, profiles, profile classes, compartments, models, repos, coding, pipelines, skills, scheduling, MCP and evolution. Each exports its interface and a `create<Name>(deps)` factory taking only what it reads, with its own test file. `createTransport`'s signature, the `Transport` type and every DTO export are unchanged.
- **Domain rules out of the transport edge.** Profile-class delete and restrict rules (`agent/core-memory/delete-profile-class.ts`, `set-profile-class-restricted.ts`), memory-scope compartment validation (`agent/evolution/find-unknown-compartment.ts`) and the chat-history projection (`agent/conversation/to-chat-history.ts`) are use cases, each taking the caller's `tx` so the identity check and the write share one snapshot.
- **Mappings.** Every store and runner outcome maps to a `TransportError` through an exhaustive ts-pattern match. `repos.add` and `repos.cloneAndAdd` share one registration path; a clone that rejects with a non-`Error` reports its text as the reason.
- **Wiring test.** `transport.test.ts` builds `createTransport` with every optional dependency omitted and pins the `routed` session default and each `*_disabled` / `*_unavailable` answer.
