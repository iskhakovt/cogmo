The two skill runtimes are named in code as the manifest names them: `wasm` and `container`, in place of the ordinals `tier1` and `tier2`. The capability flags are `wasmHardened`, `wasmMemoryBounded`, `containerEgressEnforced` and `containerTaskIsolated`; the risk reasons `wasm_unhardened`, `container_unenforced` and `container_shared_worker`; the register rejections `wasm_incompatible_dependency` and `wasm_dependency_unavailable`; the invoke rejection and cron skip reason `wasm_packages_unavailable`; the wasm child's process-group records live under `.wasm-children` in the skills directory. `classifier_log` rows written under the old names read as the new ones. The manifest's `tier: wasm | container`, the `skill_tier` enum and the directories `worker-wasm/` and `worker-sysbox/` are unchanged.
