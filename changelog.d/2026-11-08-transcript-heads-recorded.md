Append-only step 5a: every assistant row records the head of the request that produced it, and every epoch records why it opened. Nothing checks them yet; step 5b adds the checks.

- **Heads.** Each `llm-iter<N>` step hashes the request it sends (a digest chain over the system prompt, tools and messages, harness tags excluded) and returns it with the `input_transformations` the Anthropic adapter now surfaces. `persist-new-messages` and the stage turn's persist write `messages.transcript_head` on assistant rows: `{ length, base, historyStart, digest, status }`, with status `compacted` when the turn's own compaction rewrote its view without a stored summary, `diverged` when the provider reported a prefix or organization binding mismatch, else `intact`. A CHECK keeps heads off non-assistant rows.
- **Epoch reason.** `system_prompt_snapshots.reason` (`first_turn`, `configuration`, `summary`, `compaction`, `attachments`, `prefix_violation`) records why each epoch opened; existing rows are backfilled from their columns (migration 0084). A build older than this one can't open an epoch on the migrated schema.
- **Metric.** `cogmo.prompt.server_mismatches{type, reason, site}` counts each thinking block the provider dropped or let through on a failed binding check.
