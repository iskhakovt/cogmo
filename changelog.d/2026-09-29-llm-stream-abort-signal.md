`LlmProvider.chatStream` returns one async iterable ending in a `done` frame that carries the stop reason, model and usage, and `chat` and `chatStream` take an abort signal. A consumer that stops reading a stream aborts its request and ends its span, through the fallback wrapper too. An aborted call throws the signal's reason, including where an SDK would end the stream quietly, and never falls back to the next provider. The degraded-reply synthesis cancels its request at its 5-second cap.
