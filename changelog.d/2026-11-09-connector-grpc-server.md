Connectors, slice 1: cogmo serves out-of-process connectors over gRPC ([design/connectors.md](../design/connectors.md)).

- **Contract.** `packages/connector-proto` holds `cogmo.connector.v1` in two files, `session.proto` (the service, the session and the job envelopes) and `coding.proto` (the coding jobs' payloads), following the new protobuf rule (`.agents/rules/protobuf.md`), with the protobuf-es code `buf generate` writes committed. CI's `Connector contract` job runs `buf lint`, `buf breaking` against `main`, and fails when the generated code drifts from the `.proto` files.
- **Server.** With `CONNECTOR_PORT` set, `cogmo serve` listens for gRPC over h2c on `CONNECTOR_HOST` (default `0.0.0.0`, the `WEB_HOST` posture), pings each HTTP/2 connection every 30 s, and closes it after Inngest's drain, right after the web server. Only the gRPC protocol is served, and messages are read up to 4 MiB.
- **Auth.** `cogmo connector add <name> --kind coding` prints a bearer token once and stores its SHA-256 in the new `connectors` table; `rotate`, `revoke` and `list` manage it. The hub caches active connectors, refreshing every 30 s and, for an unknown token, at most every 5 s; a rotated or revoked connector's stream closes at the next refresh with `UNAUTHENTICATED`. Once three refreshes in a row have failed or stalled (90 s since the last good load started), calls get `UNAVAILABLE` and open streams close within 5 s.
- **Session hub.** One session per connector; jobs run through the `ConnectorJobs` port (`src/agent/coding/connector-jobs.ts`) with the leases (`CONNECTOR_LEASE_SECONDS`, default 120), caps and transitions in design/connectors.md → Jobs. The port's outcomes and reports are plain, frozen values the hub translates from the wire, with explicit fallbacks for codes and kinds this build does not know.
- Nothing dispatches jobs yet; coding on a connector is slice 2.
