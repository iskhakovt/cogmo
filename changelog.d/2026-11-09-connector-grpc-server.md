Connectors, slice 1: cogmo serves out-of-process connectors over gRPC ([design/connectors.md](../design/connectors.md)).

- **Contract.** `packages/connector-proto` holds `cogmo.connector.v1` as a `.proto` with its protobuf-es code generated by `buf generate` and committed. CI's `Connector contract` job runs `buf lint`, `buf breaking` against `main`, and fails when the generated code drifts from the `.proto`.
- **Server.** With `CONNECTOR_PORT` set, `cogmo serve` listens for gRPC over h2c on `CONNECTOR_HOST` (default `0.0.0.0`, the `WEB_HOST` posture), pings each HTTP/2 connection every 30 s, and closes it after Inngest's drain, right after the web server. Only the gRPC protocol is served.
- **Auth.** `cogmo connector add <name> --kind coding` prints a bearer token once and stores its SHA-256 in the new `connectors` table; `rotate`, `revoke` and `list` manage it. The hub caches active connectors, refreshing every 30 s and, for an unknown token, at most every 5 s; a rotated or revoked connector's stream closes at the next refresh with `UNAUTHENTICATED`.
- **Session hub.** One session per connector, a newer one replacing the open one (`ABORTED`). Jobs run through the `ConnectorJobs` port in `src/agent/coding/connector-jobs.ts`: dispatched, accepted, then completed, failed or cancelled from `FinishJob`, or lost, timed out or unavailable from the hub. A reconnect within `CONNECTOR_LEASE_SECONDS` (default 120) carries over the jobs its `held_job_ids` name, re-sends dispatched jobs it does not hold, and loses accepted ones it does not. Events are delivered once per `seq`. Caps per job kind, the plan and execute idle cap and the token budget cancel a job with `CancelJob`. `run` on a held `job_id` joins the wait or returns the stored outcome.
- Nothing dispatches jobs yet; coding on a connector is slice 2.
