The OpenAI-compatible adapter reads attachments as the Anthropic one does, the first slice of Append-only step 4a.

- **One text-like predicate** (`isTextLikeDocumentMediaType`, `src/llm/content.ts`) serves both adapters, so JSON, XML and YAML documents are inlined on OpenAI-compatible routes as `text/*` already was, within the 100,000-character cap. PDFs and other binary documents keep their stub.
- **Images are sized per model.** `ImageBlock` carries optional `width` and `height`, which no adapter sends. The OpenAI-compatible `countTokens` estimates each image from them by the request's model id (`estimateImageTokens`, `src/llm/image-tokens.ts`): Claude's 28-px patches capped at 4,784, Gemini's tiles of 258 with a 1,120 floor, OpenAI's 32-px patches times the model's multiplier, or OpenAI's 512-px tiles after the 2048 / 768 fit. An image without dimensions counts at its formula's most for a normalized image. The flat 85 tokens it replaces undercounted every image once later turns re-send them.
