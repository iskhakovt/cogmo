Bumped the OpenTelemetry experimental-track packages to 0.218.0:
`@opentelemetry/sdk-node`, `@opentelemetry/exporter-metrics-otlp-proto`,
`@opentelemetry/exporter-trace-otlp-proto`, and
`@opentelemetry/instrumentation-http`. The `sdk-node` bump alone (which
Dependabot proposed in #284) left the other three pinned at `^0.217.0`,
so `@opentelemetry/otlp-transformer@0.217.0` stayed resolved alongside
the new 0.218.0 from `sdk-node` and continued dragging in
`protobufjs@8.0.1` — itself carrying 6 high / 3 moderate GHSA advisories
(GHSA-75px-5xx7-5xc7 code injection, GHSA-fx83-v9x8-x52w prototype
injection, GHSA-685m-2w69-288q + GHSA-jvwf-75h9-cwgg DoS, plus three
others). Bumping the exporters and `instrumentation-http` in lockstep
collapses the 0.217 transformer branch, removes `protobufjs@8.0.1` from
the tree entirely, and drops `pnpm audit` from 13 → 6 vulnerabilities
(7 advisories closed: all six protobufjs entries plus the overlong-UTF-8
moderate). 0.218.0 ships `otlp-transformer`'s custom metrics serializer
that replaces protobufjs for that path, so the dependency footprint
shrinks rather than just shifts.

Remaining audit findings are out of scope here: two are dev-only
(`vite` via `vitest`, `esbuild` via `drizzle-kit`), two are
`@daytonaio/sdk` vendoring an older 0.207 OTel internally where we have
no override path.
