The Observer keeps going when one of its phases fails. A `new` correction that omits `matchedExistingRuleId` or `channelType` parses as `null` for both, where it used to fail the extraction on every repair retry; the JSON Schema the model receives still requires both fields. Corrections, consolidation, memory extraction and the pending-memory drain each keep their step retries, and a step that fails after them is logged at warn and reported as an empty result, so the other phases and the `evolution_events` row still run. `/reflect` still reports a failure to the user.
