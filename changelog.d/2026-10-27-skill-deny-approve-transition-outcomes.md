**A skill deploy's deny reports what it resolved, and approve's guard is its first write.** `denyPendingDeploy` is one conditional `UPDATE … WHERE status = 'pending_approval'` returning `transitioned`, `not_found`, or `not_pending` with the status the row holds. `runner.denyDeploy` returns `Result<void, DenyDeployError>`; `cogmo skills deny` exits 1 naming the miss (`deploy not found: <id>`, `deploy <id> is not pending: status is 'live'`) rather than printing `"status": "denied"` for an id it never touched; `transport.skills.denyDeploy` maps the two misses onto `skill_deploy_not_found` and `skill_deploy_not_pending` from the runner's outcome, with no read ahead of it; a missed deny is a warning in the structured log. `transport.skills.approveDeploy` recovers the same two codes from the runner's `deploy_not_found` / `deploy_not_pending` rejection by prefix.

`executeApprove` runs its `pending_approval → live` UPDATE before the `skills` projection. This is hardening: under REPEATABLE READ a resolution committed after approve's snapshot raises `40001` at its first UPDATE on either table and the transactor retries from a fresh snapshot, whose pre-read refuses the row (`denied` after a deny, `live` after another approve), so the earlier order could not commit a projection without its transition. With the transition first, a zero-row match also provably writes nothing.

Two more hardenings from the design review of the lifecycle. `register` deletes its feature branch after the commit, best-effort, instead of inside the transaction: a register whose commit fails after `update-ref` moved `main` keeps its branch, so repeating it converges instead of rejecting `branch_not_found`; a delete that fails is a warning, and the branch re-registers as `no_op`. `mirrorMainToRemote` catches every failure — reading `origin`, resolving the identity, the push — so a committed deploy never comes back as a thrown error.

`design/skills.md` gains the deploy lifecycle (`[confirmed]`): the unit, its states, a transition table, and a failure-mode table that names, with `todo.md` entries, the residuals the review found in the existing code: a post-`update-ref` commit failure followed by a deny leaves a denied commit on `main`; rollback rewinds the shared `main` past other skills' deploys; two registers of one skill can insert two pending rows; approve re-enables a skill deregistered meanwhile (decision: approve keeps `disabled` when the skill's current sha has a `live` row — to implement). Store tests cover each deny outcome and pin that approve writes nothing when its guarded UPDATE matches no row — a Proxy on the transaction denies the row right before that statement; CLI tests pin exit 1 for both misses.
