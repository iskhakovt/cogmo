Fix a month-rollover flake in the Hindsight evolution integration tests (`backfillProfileClass`, `migrateUntaggedMemories`). Hindsight stamps each extracted fact with a `(happened in <Month> <Year>)` temporal suffix — derived from the *current* date — before embedding it, but the llmock embedding matcher didn't date-normalize that suffix. A fixture recorded one month therefore stopped matching the next, surfacing as `503 no_fixture_match` on `/v1/embeddings`. A shared `(happened in <Month> <Year>)` -> `(happened in [WHEN])` normalizer now runs on both the chat (`normalizeContent`) and embedding (`embeddingInput`) match paths, and the one affected fixture is updated to the normalized token.
