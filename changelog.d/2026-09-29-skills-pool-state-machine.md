The skills warm pool runs on a pure state machine (`worker-sysbox/pool-state.ts`) that `SysboxWorkerPool` shells, like the worker channels. Every transition ends in one level-triggered `reconcile`: it grants idle workers to the oldest waiters and spawns for each waiter and for any shortfall to `min`, so no waiter waits on nothing whichever event queued it, and a grant the worker refuses requeues its waiter. A waiter's worker counts toward `min`, so freed slots spawn only for the waiters queued. A worker's `dead` resolves with who ended it, `host` or `worker`, so the crash-loop cap needs no side set, and every acquire pending at disposal fails as `disposed before worker available`. While the crash-loop cap holds, a waiter with no busy worker to wait for waits on one probe spawn at a time: a probe that lives serves it and ends the loop, and one that dies early fails it. Once workers live again, the next acquire is served, whatever `min` is, with no sweep or restart. Boot creates exactly `min` workers. A transition table, seeded properties over the machine, and the pool scenarios and fuzz cover it.
