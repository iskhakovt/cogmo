**Telegram drops every update from an unknown sender before any handler runs.** A grammY middleware (`sender-gate.ts`), registered ahead of commands, callback queries and message handlers, asks `transport.senders.admits(handle)`; on a `mapped` channel a sender with no `user_identities` row gets nothing back — no reply, typing indicator, download or upload, including for `/start` and button taps — and the drop is logged at info with the handle only. A bot is publicly discoverable, so any reply confirms it is live and tells a stranger what it accepts, and work done before the check (downloads into the attachment store) is an abuse path. A stranger in a group chat with an active session no longer reaches its conversation or an open `/profile` dialog.

`senders.admits` matches the channel's identity mode exhaustively: `fixed` admits everyone; `mapped` and `create` admit a handle with an identity row (nothing auto-creates identities yet). A missing channel row admits no one and warns. A DB error during the check drops that update unprocessed and logs the handle and update id, not its content: failing open would answer strangers.
