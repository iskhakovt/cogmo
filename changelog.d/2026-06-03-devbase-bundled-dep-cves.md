The `cogmo-devbase` image installs pnpm via npm (matching the workspace `packageManager`) instead of `corepack prepare` and upgrades npm, clearing the corepack- and npm-bundled-dependency CVEs (tar, picomatch, minimatch, brace-expansion, ip-address) Trivy flagged on the published image. `docker-bake.hcl` centralises the task images' toolchain pins (uv, pnpm, npm, Claude Code) as build args; the devbase Dockerfile keeps matching defaults so it still builds standalone, and a version-pin consistency test keeps the two from drifting. The workspace pnpm bumps to 11.5.1 and the transitive `tmp` dependency is pinned `>=0.2.6`.
