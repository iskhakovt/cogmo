Surface explicit refusal signals at the `LlmProvider` interface. The `StopReason` union gains a `"refusal"` variant; `anthropic.ts` decodes `stop_reason: "refusal"` (the canonical signal on recent Claude models) and `openai-compat.ts` decodes `finish_reason: "content_filter"` on success-path choices. A new `RefusalError` (in `src/llm/fallback.ts`) wraps 400-class content-policy errors from the OpenAI SDK — `BadRequestError` with `code: "content_policy_violation"` (OpenAI) or `"responsible_ai_policy_violation"` (Azure). `isRetriableProviderError` reports `RefusalError` as non-retriable so `FallbackLlmProvider` propagates it untouched; silent re-routing across providers on a policy refusal is the wrong shape per the design.

Per `design/agent-resilience.md` Class C, refusal detection is scoped to Anthropic-direct + OpenAI-direct — but the same OpenAI adapter serves OpenAI-compat shims (OpenRouter, Venice, xAI), and there's no clean adapter-time way to distinguish them from the base URL. Compat providers ride along when they happen to emit an OpenAI-shaped refusal signal; the design acknowledges this as best-effort.

Foundation only. The in-loop classifier that consumes the new signal — emitting a refusal-specific degraded reply and a `conversation/degraded` event — ships in PR 5.
